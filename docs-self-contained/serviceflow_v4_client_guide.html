§ 01How Your Retainer Works

Your retainer gives you a set number of credits each month. You spend those credits on services — things like SEO work, Google Ads management, content, analytics, or any other services in your plan. Think of it like a monthly budget: new credits arrive on the 1st of every month, and you decide how to spend them.

Unused credits do not carry over to the next month. Each month starts fresh. This keeps things clean and avoids complicated running totals building up over time. The only exceptions are very specific situations covered under Off Plan work — and each has clear rules explained below.

The simple versionEach month: credits arrive → you select services → we do the work → credits are spent. Simple, transparent, no surprises. We review each quarter and plan the next 3 months work together.

On Plan & Off Plan — The Two Umbrellas

Everything in your retainer falls into one of two categories:

On Plan
Your Quarterly Plan
Services agreed at your quarterly review and selected on the Quarterly Order Form. Planned, predictable, and pre-approved — the heart of your retainer.
Off Plan
Everything Else
Anything outside your agreed quarterly plan — corrections, urgent work, or important new tasks. Has specific rules, credit limits, and scheduling implications depending on type.

Off Plan work comes in three types, each with different implications for your plan and your credits:

Off Plan · Type 1 · Most common
Revisions & Supplementary Work

Small corrections to delivered work, or smaller additional items that sit alongside your plan — without disrupting it. Revisions run concurrently with your existing scheduled work and cause no disruption to your plan whatsoever.

Credit Rules — The Short Version

A few rules apply across all work types. The full detail is in your contract and Schedule E — this is the plain-English summary.

Monthly credits
e.g. 150 / month
Arrive on the 1st. Do not carry over — each month is a fresh start.
Off Plan credit limit
Max 2× monthly
Emergency and priority work is capped at twice your monthly allocation (e.g. 300 for a 150/month plan). Revisions have a separate, lower limit.
Revision — next quarter
1 month or £200
You can use from your next quarter's allocation for revisions — maximum of 200 credits or £200 if paid separately.
Payment method
One method only
Credits or separate payment — never a mix within the same request. See your contract for your specific discount rate on paid work.
No rolloverCredits do not accumulate. If you don't use your 150 credits in March, they don't appear in April. April always starts with your standard monthly allocation and nothing more. This is intentional — it keeps your balance simple and your planning clear.
Credits cannot be purchased separatelyPurchasing individual credits outside your retainer would create complexity that ultimately costs you more money. If we allowed à la carte credit purchases, we'd need to manage custom credit arrangements for multiple clients — which means higher overhead costs that get passed back to you in higher prices. Instead, we keep it simple and fair: options to upgrade for best value, pay separately with a discount, or in limited cases use from a future quarter.

Research has shown that clients who stay on retainers, upgrade strategically, and build long-term relationships get dramatically better results than clients who treat it transactionally. The model only works if we can count on stable, predictable relationships. In return, you get better value, dedicated capacity, and results that compound over time. It's a genuine win-win.
Plan upgrades & downgradesIf you need more credits and are considering a plan upgrade, the same scheduling rules apply — upgrading by the 7th is the only way to guarantee it takes effect this month. It may still be possible to upgrade your plan if you request it by the 15th, but this is not guaranteed. Frequency of upgrades and downgrades is governed by Schedule E of your contract to ensure stability for both parties.
§ 02Your Quarterly Order Form

At the end of each quarter we'll meet and discuss your priorities, presenting and agreeing in principle the next quarter's work. Following our meeting, at the start of the new quarter, you'll receive access to your Quarterly Order Form — a simple form in your client portal that lets you formally authorise which service/s you'd like for the next three months.

This form is the main way your planned (ON PLAN) work gets agreed and scheduled. Once you've submitted it, we receive your selections automatically and schedule your work. No back-and-forth needed.

Quarterly Order Form is live nowYour Quarterly Order Form is already available in your client portal. You don't need to wait — when a new quarter opens, your form will appear ready to complete.
How to Use the Quarterly Order Form
📋
Open the form in your portal
Your Quarterly Order Form appears in your portal space at the start of each quarter. It's personalised to your plan — only services available to you will show up.
💡
Review the suggested strategies (optional)
We often suggest named strategies — for example, "Strategy A: Google Ads Focus" or "Strategy B: SEO & Content". Each strategy groups services together by theme and suggests which month to schedule each one. You're not locked in to a strategy — it's just a starting point to help you decide.
🗓️
Select your services
Generally for each month in the quarter, you'll select one service from the dropdown. Bigger services may span multiple months, depending on the credit total and your plan. Each service shows its credit cost. The form keeps a running total so you can see how much you're spending. The key is stay within your quarter credit total. Some services show a ✨ bonus — this means the credit cost is reduced by that amount for this selection.
✅
Check your total and submit
When you're happy with your selections, tick the authorisation checkbox and click Submit. You'll see a confirmation message on screen and you'll receive a confirmation in your portal. We'll have your selections instantly and your work is scheduled automatically.
Submission deadlineEach quarter has a deadline for submitting your form — usually the 7th of the first month of the quarter. This is shown on the form itself. Submitting early means your first month's work can start on time. If you miss the deadline, contact us in the portal and we'll do our best to accommodate you.
Bonus credits From time to time some services on the form carry one time bonus credits — shown as a ✨ next to the service. This means that service costs you fewer net credits than the standard price (for this quarter only). For example, a service might cost 200 credits normally, but with a 50-credit bonus your net cost is only 150 credits. The form handles this automatically in your total — no action needed from you.

If you need to change your selections after submitting, you can resubmit the form. Please contact us via the portal to confirm your choices are from the latest submission so we can void any previous ones.

Quarterly Review Meetings

Every three months we have a review meeting — this may be in person or via video call depending on what works best.

Before your review meeting, it's worth thinking about: what's working well, what you'd like more or less of, and any new priorities or projects coming up. We'll also look at your credit usage together so there are no surprises.

After the meeting, we'll confirm your Quarterly Order Form for the new quarter and you'll be able to see everything that's planned. We'll also discuss whether your current plan level is still the right fit, and whether any of your perks or bundles need updating.

§ 03Tracking Your Work & the Monthly Reset
What Happens to Each Piece of Work

Every service you request goes through a simple journey. Here's what each status means when you see it:

Pending Approval
We're reviewing your request. Credits not yet affected. Usually resolved within a few hours.
Approved
Request confirmed. Credits are reserved (set aside) but not yet spent. Work hasn't started yet.
In Progress
We're actively working on this. Credits are spent at this point. Work has begun.
Completed
Delivered and finished. Credits were spent when work started. You should have received the deliverable.
On Hold
Paused — usually because urgent work came in that needed to take priority and these credits were spent elsewhere. Work can resume once re-requested.
When are credits actually spent?Credits are only deducted when work moves to "In Progress".
What Happens on the 1st of Each Month

On the 1st of every month, your new credit allocation arrives automatically. You'll see your new balance updated in your portal. Here's what that looks like:

What happens each month
1st
Your new credits arrive. Your monthly allocation is added to your balance and visible in your portal.
1st–7th
Priority window for new requests. The 7th is our soft deadline for new monthly requests — submitting in this window gives us the best chance of fitting everything in this month.
Mid
Work progresses. Scheduled services move through Approved → In Progress → Completed. You can view current work in the reports area of the portal.
Month end
Invoice for the month. An invoice covering the month's completed services is generated and sent. Your next credit load arrives on the 1st of the new month.
After the 7th — what it means in practiceWe can still accept new requests after the 7th, but the window is narrowing. Between the 7th and 15th we'll do our best to complete work this month, but it's not guaranteed. After the 15th, any new request is a best-effort situation — there's a good chance it will need to start the following month. If something is genuinely urgent, let us know as soon as possible whatever the date.

Off Plan · Emergency
Something genuinely urgent has come up outside your quarterly plan
⌄
You need work done urgently — something that can't wait until next quarter's review and needs to be scheduled as soon as possible.
01

❌ After 15th: Best effort — may need to start next month
02

🪙 Current plan credits — up to a maximum of 2× your monthly allocation (e.g. 300 credits for a 150/month plan)

📈 Plan upgrade — gives you more credits to cover higher-cost services. Must be requested by the 7th for this month. Upgrade and downgrade frequency is governed by Schedule E of your contract.

Because an emergency usually means your situation has changed, the entire remaining quarter is treated as needing a fresh look. All paused months will need to be re-requested — this keeps everything properly authorised and scheduled for both sides. We'll guide you through this once the emergency work is resolved.
04

Revisions & Supplementary Work
Off Plan · Revision
You need a small correction or a supplementary item alongside your existing plan
⌄
Revisions cover two things: corrections to work we've already delivered, and smaller supplementary items that sit alongside your plan without disrupting it. This is the best type of Off Plan work — no disruption, no quarter reset.
01
Revisions are the right route for:
Corrections: Tweaking or adjusting something already delivered — a colour, a line of copy, a data point in a report.

Supplementary additions: Adding a smaller item that runs alongside your plan. For example, your plan includes website copy — you.d also like a few text amends to a landing page added on top. This can be handled as a revision with zero disruption to your existing schedule.
02
Use the revision request form in your portal. Revisions run concurrently with your On Plan work — your planned services continue completely undisturbed.
✅ By 7th: Completion this month guaranteed
☑️ 7th–15th: Likely but not guaranteed this month
❌ After 15th: Best effort — may need to start next month
03
Payment — one method only, never a mix:
🪙 Current plan credits — if you have enough in your balance this month

🗓️ Use from your next quarter's allocation — if your credits are exhausted. Maximum of 200 credits, or £200 if paying separately. This amount is deducted from your next quarter's available credits. Not available if your retainer is in a notice period.

💳 Separate payment — at your contracted discount rate, collected via GoCardless/FreeAgent. Credits are always the most cost-effective option, but paid work at your contracted rate is available when needed.
04
We'll confirm the plan with you — what the revision covers, when it'll be done, and the credit or payment arrangement. ✅ Your quarterly plan remains completely unaffected.
Taking a Retainer Break
Scenario
You need to pause your retainer for a month
⌄
Life gets busy. If you need to pause your retainer for a month, we can accommodate that — subject to your plan's break allowance and notice requirements.
01
Check your eligibility before requesting:
Breaks are usually available after your first 6 months with us.
Your contract will confirm the number of breaks per year.
You need to give at least 30 days' notice before the month you want to pause. For example, to pause May, you need to let us know by around 1st April.
If you haven't met the notice period, contact us anyway — we can discuss whether an exception is possible.
02
Use the retainer break request form in your portal. Tell us which month you'd like to pause and any context that's helpful for us to know.
03
We'll confirm and process your break. Any work planned for that month will be stood down, and that month's retainer fee will be refunded. We'll send you a portal confirmation listing everything that was stood down and what you're being refunded.
04
Stood-down work does not automatically reschedule. When you return, we'll discuss what you need — either at your next quarterly review or by submitting a new request. Think of it as a fresh start for that month.
05
When you return, your credits reload as normal on the 1st of your return month and you can submit new requests straight away.

§ 06Getting Help

Most things can be handled through the forms in your client portal. Here's how to get the right kind of help:

Best for most things
Use the relevant form in your portal
For all requests — urgent work, breaks, balance checks, extra work, revisions. There's a form for each type of request and it gets things to us quickly.
General questions
Use the AI assistant in your portal
Good for general how-to questions about your retainer — key dates, how credits work, what the different work types are. The assistant can't access your specific account info (balance, history etc).
If needed
Send a portal message
For anything that doesn't fit a form, send us a message directly in your portal. We aim to reply within 48 hours (office hours).
What the AI assistant can and can't doThe AI assistant in your portal is great for general questions about how your retainer works — things like "when do my credits arrive?" or "what's the difference between planned and emergency work?". It can't tell you your specific balance, what services you had last month, or any information specific to your account. For those, use the balance check form.
Policy questionsIf you have questions about what's included in your retainer, intellectual property, termination terms, or anything legal — please refer to your retainer contract and any accompanying schedules.
